# STEM Umbrella 
### Excelling Knowledge Transfer

STEM Umbrella is a B2B & B2C company group, altogether provides educational ecosystem for students, teachers, schools and states to gain active engagement in STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics) subjects. 

## STEM License
Our services and products are compliance with STEM Umbrella License which comes in four versions: 
- Tier 1 - STEM Schools (5 years contract, first year free)
- Tier 2 - STEM Teachers (3 years contract, first years free)
- Tier 3 - STEM Lancers (1 year, first month free)
- Tier 4 - STEM Students (Free) 

License type give access to product and services of STEM Umbrella

## Product and Services
All the products and services delivered by STEM Umbrella, developed and maintained by STEM Team. 
- STEM Consultancy - Guidance for STEM Career 
- STEM Library - Knowledge-Semantics database [4]
- STEM Presentor - Tool for teachers (Laptop based) and students (Mobile app). [For Tier 1,2]
- STEM Market - Apply learnings to learn [3]
- STEM Kit - Digital Accessories and connectivity
- STEM Labs - Experimental infrastructure (Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Computer) [1,2]
